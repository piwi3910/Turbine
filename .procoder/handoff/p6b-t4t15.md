## Pricing model (decision "6b Task 4: planner copy bytes", A)

The planner prices a copy at its encoded bytes (`PlanInputs.copy_bytes`). `TransferEngine`
observes every completed non-`Compress` copy at `req.bytes`, the bytes it moved (t15's
`max(bytes, from_bytes, to_bytes)` is gone). The bandwidth sample is now bytes over
(duration − latency estimate), and it's taken only when that is at least half the copy. The
old whole-duration sample made small, latency-bound compressed copies drag the per-byte rate
down (a full block priced 51 % slow), and full-size L2 blocks then priced above recompute.
The simulator's copy time is latency + `req.bytes`/bandwidth, so estimates seeded at its
costs stay there. Tests `transfer::tests::{estimates_are_per_encoded_byte,
mixed_copy_sizes_keep_latency_out_of_the_rate}` (both red before the fix).

kv_sim `ladder_under_pinned_pressure` recomputed tokens, ladder on / off: merged with the
double count 129856 / 150784; `req.bytes` observation alone 164944 / 150784 (the ladder
lost: that led to the latency fix); reconciled 145488 / 147840; with GREEN step-up

## GREEN step-up (user decision 2026-09-30, A)

`ladder_tick` steps a rung up only when `pressure == Green` and the tier has been below
low water for the dwell. The dwell clock runs on fill alone, so a tier that stayed below low
water through RED may step up in the first GREEN ticks (step 5118, GREEN starts at 5100).
New assertion: no step-up while the pinned state is not GREEN. It failed on the old tree
(step 4147, ORANGE). The fixture now has 9 changes, with step-ups only at GREEN (5118, 5611,
6091), and the steady-YELLOW stretch has none. Mutations (reverted from snapshots): drop the
GREEN condition → FAIL; drop the YELLOW `low_water` stop → FAIL.

## Open

1. Eviction score retrieval pricing (a design question, not decided). `score_one` still
   prices the retrieval at the L0 block size, like the `memory` term's `size_bytes`. That
   overstates a compressed copy's retrieval under per-encoded-byte rates. Pricing only the
   retrieval at encoded bytes breaks `per_tier_formats` (L1 holds 7 instead of more than 8
   blocks). Options: A) keep it as it is (conservative; the stack's pre-t15 behaviour);
   B) price both retrieval and memory at the copy's encoded size in its tier (consistent;
   the policy's value changes for lossy tiers, and `demote_min_value` is absolute);
   C) price retrieval only at encoded size and re-pin `per_tier_formats`.
   Recommendation: B, as its own task with the policy tests re-checked.
2. The rung can jump more than one step in one tick (`l0 → tq4`) when a copy that is already
   lossier than the rung is compressed. Per-block moves are still one rung; only the rung
   gauge jumps. Seen in intermediate runs, not in the current fixture.

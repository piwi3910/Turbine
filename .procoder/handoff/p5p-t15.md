in LDS (double buffered, 62 KB → one block per CU), 8 waves of 64×64, the output tile written through LDS as 16-byte
chunks (`Params::c_vec`). It chains WMMAs over natural k with `decode8_scaled` weights, exactly like the Large tile,
so rows are bitwise identical; the batch-invariance test (1,031-row call vs 1/7/128) crosses both tiles.

## Open (for the lead)

1. **The eval gate cannot run as specified.** Two attempts on the ladder config plus `kv.gpu.max_bytes=4GiB`, 32 fillers, c16.
   Neither gave a report; nothing was committed.
   - With default thresholds the eval stayed GREEN, so the ladder never ran (lossy ratio 0.000; served only its 726,723 prompt
     tokens).
   - With `reliability.pressure.thresholds.kv_utilization=[0.03,0.06,0.9,0.97]` it ran at ORANGE, and still no ladder action
     happened. The sweep starts at the lowest tier, L2, which the eval leaves empty. L1 only compresses once L2 sits on a lower
     rung, and the prefix is in L1 at `l0`. Admission also queued items on `kv_reservation`, and item 6 failed with 503
     `queue_timeout`, so the run aborted.
   - Options:
     - A: run the gate on the ladder config with `kv.nvme.enabled=false` on both sides, so L1 is the lowest tier, and add a
       runner flag `--filler-concurrency <n>` (`benches/turbine-bench`, eval runner) so the fillers create real ORANGE pressure
       (16 × 23 blocks > 292 L0 blocks). Thresholds stay at their defaults.
     - B: a diagnostics endpoint that forces one ladder sweep at a given state (new interface, test-only build).
     - C: judge the ladder by the per-rung gates already passed (L1 `fp8_e4m3`, L1 `tq4`) plus the byte-equality test
       `ladder_rewrites_run_on_device_lanes`. Weaker: an `l0 → fp8 → tq4` chain quantizes twice, and no gate has measured that.
   - Recommendation: A. It measures the real chain, needs no new server interface, and keeps the recipe's pair rule (same
     settings on both sides).
2. **L2 loses a copy on `no_room`** (turbine-kv, not edited here). `L2NvmeTier::store` removes the old index entry and frees its
   slot before it looks for a slot of the new size. On `Full` the copy is gone, and `on_copy_failed` evicts it with reason
   `tier_degraded`. L1's `put` keeps the old copy. The spec edge case says the copy keeps its format.
   - Fix (for the turbine-kv owner): allocate first, then free the old slot. The server test pins today's behaviour
     (`!l2.contains(&key)`), so it must be flipped with the fix.
3. **L1 rewrites spin on `no_room`** (slab fragmentation, first builder's Open 3). With decision 3 A (no slab change) the L1 ladder
   does nothing useful and costs device time.
   - Options:
     - a: back off a block, or a tier's sweep, after `no_room` until a slab frees.
     - b: smaller L1 slabs.
     - c: the sweep picks victims slab by slab, so a slab empties and is reformatted.
   - Recommendation: a first (cheap and bounded), then c.
4. **The soak cannot exercise the ladder.** `overload-soak.sh` sends random prompts (`--prompt-words-range 64..6000`), so nothing is
   reused or demoted.
   - Options:
     - a: add a shared-prefix share to the soak's load (for example `--shared-prefix-words` on part of the requests, if
       `turbine-bench` supports it outside the multi-turn profile, else a small bench change). The 8 checks stay as they are.
     - b: add a multi-turn leg to the soak script after the overload phase.
     - c: amend the AC to "the ladder soak passes and the multi-turn A/B shows ladder actions".
   - Recommendation: a. It keeps one soak and exercises the ladder under real overload.
5. The two eval attempts ran on 4e49409, before the merges. Their blocker (tier order, admission at low thresholds) is
   structural and does not depend on drift. The lab was closed by the lead before a re-check could run.
